Apply the `next-steps` skill (`~/.claude/skills/next-steps/SKILL.md`) to the work just
completed, or to whatever the args name.

1. **What this means** — 2–3 plain sentences: what changed and why it matters to the
   reader. No jargon, no mechanism-by-mechanism recap.
2. **Next steps** — at most three, verb first, each naming an owner (*you* / *me* /
   *waiting on X*) and a rough size. Ordered by what to do first.
3. If nothing is needed from them, say exactly that rather than inventing work. After a
   failure, the step is the recovery action, not "investigate."
4. Put the steps last. Nothing goes below them.
5. Apply the crystal-clear test before sending: reading only the footer, could someone
   act correctly without scrolling up?
